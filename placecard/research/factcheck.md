Method: every cited URL was fetched with WebFetch. Where a site blocked me (moma.org returned 403 for the Rodchenko page) I used independent search results quoting the museum text. "Weak relevance" means the item does not show a flat card standing by itself.

## Adjacent items (checked briefly)
- Karlis Johansons / Karl Ioganson, Cold Structures 1921: confirmed that self-stressed bar-and-cable structures were exhibited in Moscow in 1921, none survived, and reconstructions were made in 2015 (Wikipedia). "Cold joints (crosses)" not verified. Tensegrity does not give a flat card.
- Daniel Spoerri, tableaux pieges: confirmed, with Restaurant Spoerri in Dusseldorf 1968-72 (Apollo). Dinner-as-medium only.
- Takenobu Igarashi: letterforms, correctly flagged as weak.
- Zim & Zou, Snelson, Miura-ori, Kobro, Sutnar, Caro, kenzan holders: unchecked, as the researcher states.

## Letterform / blobby / receipt-paper check
- Letterforms: Bataille (ABC3D) is explicitly an alphabet. Drop. Igarashi (adjacent) is also letterforms.
- Blobby organic outlines: no reference is about them, but Du Pasquier ("pebbles"), Brancusi (eggs and birds), Yoshioka (honeycomb) and Shlian's "pebble" suggestion all push toward soft organic shapes if copied literally. Keep to the mechanism and not the silhouette.
- Receipt/ticket paper: no reference uses it. Orta's fabric runner overlaps the already-used receipt-paper runner, which is one more reason to drop it.

## Summary
Confirmed 4 (Du Pasquier, Yoshioka, Clark, Fischli & Weiss). Corrected 8 (Gabo, Albers, Demaine, Rodchenko, Teruya, Shlian, Brancusi, Serra). Unverified 1 (Komagata). Drop 5 (Bataille, Miyake, Pacovska, Orta, Chicago). Strongest for "a flat card that stands by itself": Gabo (slots), Albers and Demaine (single-sheet self-forming), Clark (hinged plates). Weak and best used as mood only: Du Pasquier, Yoshioka, Brancusi, Serra, Fischli & Weiss, Rodchenko (hangs), Teruya (volume bag).
