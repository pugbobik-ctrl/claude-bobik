# D3 v2 - One sheet for the whole table (skewed straight-cut mosaic)

Files: d3_dieline_SRA3.svg (full sheet 1:1, landscape 450 x 320), d3_piece_detail.svg, d3_assembled.svg (front + back views, section, lock detail), d3_scaling.svg, d3_verify.txt.

## Changes
- Outlines: the S-curves are gone. All cut lines are straight; the 8 vertical dividers are inclined 6-12 deg (rows (6, 9, 12), (-10, -7, -12), (8, 12, 7) deg), so 12 visibly different quadrilaterals (12 distinct outlines, widths 98-117 at the leaves). Still zero waste: every edge is shared, tab and notch nest; verified no overlap, no gap.
- Grain: the mosaic is rotated (4 pieces along the 450 mm side, 3 rows along 320) so every crease runs along the long side = long grain of a standard SRA3. Piece 106 x 101: leaf 34.5 (was 36), flap 12, B 20 (the critic's B 21 would end 0.32 mm from the foot; 20 ends 1.32 mm short), tent height 32.8, depth 21.32, closure 21.32 = 12 + 9.32. Gripper margin 13 on the short edge.
- Creases: ALL five scored from the back (one-sided flatbed creasing). Crease 5 (hidden tab fold-over) folds against its score. Crease pitch >= 3.0 (tab riser 3.0, fold-over 3.5, tab 6.5 -> notch 6.5 deep; slit still clears the notch by 3.8 mm). Crease positions 20 / 54.5 / 89 / 101 / 104.
- Text: TABLE 4 . SEAT nn on F and DINNER . date on K at 4.9 mm (3.5 mm caps); name higher on the leaf (14-19 mm up the slope = 13-18 mm above the table, was 7).
- Heavy line: one 2.4 mm black line runs along the rear leaf (K) of all 12 pieces (row 1 left to right, joined in the trim margin, row 2 right to left, row 3 left to right). Each tent shows a fragment on its back; the line is whole only when the 12 pieces are laid back into the sheet outline (and gives staff a reason to count the cards back).
- Through-cut, no nicks, tray delivery (critic: 60 nicks would leave burrs). Digital-press weight note: use 350 g (0.44, slit 0.60) if the press stops at 300-350 g. Pre-coloured lemon board, no bleed.

## Production
One steel-rule die, cutting + creasing on one side. Laser: kerf 0.2 on shared lines gives 0.2 gaps and edge scorch on lemon. Plotter: 10 odd nodes, at least 5 knife passes, oscillating knife (drag knife needs overcut at the tab corners). Assembly: 5 folds + tuck, estimate 40-60 s for the first dozen; assembled tents are 33 mm high so they are assembled at the venue.

## Risks
Slit width 0.70 for one layer; tab fold-over fiddly; the whole-table idea survives only in the heavy line and the sheet numbering.
