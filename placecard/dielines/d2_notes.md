# D2 v2 - Form and its void, compact (lead direction)

Files: d2_dieline.svg (lemon; hidden layer "artwork_reverse" on the same die), d2_dieline_reverse.svg (black card, lemon type visible), d2_assembled.svg, d2_assembled_reverse.svg, d2_verify.txt.

## What changed from v1 (and why)
- Card 105 x 118 (was 105 x 148, -20 % area): front strip 20, S 32 high, web 8, b = 40, P 44.29 long at 44.3 deg, lip 6, rear margin 7.7. Square trim, no rounded corners, no banner, no TABLE/SEAT strip on the flat card; only one quiet line "DINNER 10.10.2026" (3.5 mm caps) in the front strip.
- S: circle segment chord 90, height 32, R 47.6 (the review's "R 33.7" is a slip: (45^2+32^2)/(2*32) = 47.6). Gentler arc than v1, name zone width 74.8 at its cap top.
- Strut stiffened with 2 side flanges (5 mm, folded 90 deg up/back, away from S and table). Flat strut: I 0.32 mm4, Euler 3.25 N, push at the crown ~2.9 N (300 gf). Flanged: I 36 mm4 (112 x). Flanges run from 6 mm after c2 to 8 mm before c3 (crease pitch >= 3), end in 4 short slits with O1 reliefs. Why not twin struts: each strip is narrower (I ~ width; two 12 mm strips = 0.25 mm4, 1.3 N each) and needs rungs or two hooks; flanges keep ONE part, ONE hook, all creases scored from the same face (flange creases fold against the score, hidden).
- Relief: O1.8 cut at both ends of c1 (S) and c2 (P), so the first lift does not tear the hinge corner.
- Lip no longer clips type: the lip covers v 26-32 on S; table/seat line cap top is at v 21.0 (5 mm clear), name baseline v 9. Table/seat now on S, 4.9 mm type = 3.5 mm caps (was 2.2-2.5 mm).
- All creases c1 c2 c3 valley, scored from the printed face; flange creases dash-dot (against the score).
- P length tolerance: +-0.5 mm = +-1.0 deg on S lean (73.98 / 75.00 / 76.03). Fold take-up (0.3-0.4 mm per 135 deg fold) lies inside that window; test strut lengths 44.0 / 44.3 / 44.6 on the first article.

## Reverse colourway (second artwork, same die)
Black through-dyed board, lemon type #feed95 (d2_dieline_reverse.svg, d2_assembled_reverse.svg). Lemon type on black needs an opaque ink: screen print, hot-foil or offset opaque yellow; a digital press cannot print light ink on dark. On a lemon table runner the two voids read as lemon shapes beside their black twins (positive / negative). Critic's variant (lemon card, black reverse, black runner) uses the same die: duplex black on the back of the card.

## Board and press
Pre-coloured lemon board: no flood print, no bleed, fold cracks stay yellow. 350-400 g/m2 (0.44-0.50). Digital presses mostly stop at 300-350 g: use 350 g (flanges, 0.44) or litho / screen for 400 g. Grain parallel to the 105 mm side = parallel to c1 c2 c3.

## How it stands
Closed triangle base / S (75 deg) / P (44.3 deg), lip hooks over the crown: S crown (8.28, 30.91) from c1 = P tip (verified). Pull on S: P in tension, hook holds. Push on S: flanged P no longer buckles (Euler 365 N); the limit is then the lip / crease c2, to be measured on the first article. COM inside the footprint; sliding (about 2 gf) comes before tipping.

## Imposition / production
Cards stand with their 105 mm side along the 450 mm side: 4 x 105 + 3 x 3 = 429, 2 x 118 + 3 = 239: 8 per SRA3, long grain parallel to the creases. Steel-rule die with cutting + creasing rules, creases from one side only (all scored from the printed face); crease runs 0.5 mm past the cut as tear stop. Laser: kiss-score, edge scorch visible on lemon.

## Risks
Hook lip is still the fiddly step (staff can pre-assemble; cards then do not stack flat); flange slits are small (0.5 mm rule min.); black-through board dust / white edge on cut; test strut length.
