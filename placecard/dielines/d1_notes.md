Files: d1_dieline.svg, d1_sheet_SRA3.svg (15 sets + slot test strip), d1_assembled.svg, d1_sail_variant.svg, d1_verify.txt.

## Changes
- Name raised: baseline z 27 (cap top 32.1), ABOVE plate B. B is now 22 high at the centre, 26.4 at the ends, so it never rises above the name baseline; the eyebrow line TABLE 4 . SEAT nn (4.9 mm = 3.5 mm caps) sits above the name on A (cap top 38.8, dome 42.3 at its edge). One line, on A, readable by the seated guest; B is edge-on to her and now only carries DINNER / 10 OCT (3.5 mm caps) for neighbours. No two-box "ticket stub".
- B shortened: 64 wide (arms 32; was 88). Footprint 100 x 64, COM z 18.4, rhombus inradius 27.0 mm -> tip-over 55.7 deg (v1 61). Asymmetric option (front 18 / rear 38) not drawn.
- Slot depth A 11.0, B floor 10.4: bottoming gap 0.6 (was 0.2, below die / laser tolerance). Play angle 1.04 / 0.99 deg, A top sways 0.78 mm.
- Slot spec (net 0.70 for 0.50 board): steel rule: a single 2 pt rule only slits (fibre springback, the critic's 0.35-0.55 opening is not verified by me): ask for (a) 3 pt (1.07) blunt slot rule or (b) two 2 pt rules 0.70 apart with a stripping pin; the first article decides. Laser: draw 0.50 (kerf 0.20); scorch is visible on lemon. Knife plotter: not suitable.
- Slot test strip on every SRA3: four coupons with 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.80 / 0.90 slits (slip in a scrap of the real board).
- Slot ends: O1.4 relief circle (tangent to the nominal depth) and a 0.6 x 45 deg lead-in chamfer instead of the 2.3 x 1.5 flare.
- Imposition: 15 sets + 4 coupons, B (turned 180 deg) still nests over A's dome (same R 119.1, 4.5 mm gap); material use 58.8 % because B is smaller (spare room could take extra B or the sail).
- Seat numbers vary per set on the sheet. Pre-coloured lemon board, no bleed. 400 g duplex: litho / screen / flat-bed digital; presses limited to 300-350 g need 350 g and a 0.60 slot.

## Sail variant (sketch, d1_sail_variant.svg)
Low plate A (100 x 34, name at z 13.5) + tall fin B (72 high at the front edge, 18 mm toe in front, 74 mm rear arm, dip-arc top edge, numeral cap 55 mm = 77 mm type) behind it. A rests against the fin face (gap 0.2); no slot on B, slot in A 10.0 / toe 9.4. COM y 21.6, z 25.9, tip-over 49 deg. Weakest joint: A is only held backwards by the fin (test first). Not tooled, imposition not drawn.

## Risks
Slot fit remains the critical dimension; two loose parts per guest; B edge-on to the guest (by design).
